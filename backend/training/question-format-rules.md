Statement-based questions:
- Start with "Consider the following statements:".
- Use at least 4 numbered statements.
- Put every statement on its own line.
- Use combination options such as "1 and 2 only", "2, 3 and 4 only", "1, 3 and 4 only", or "All four statements".

One-liner questions:
- Keep the stem direct.
- Avoid unnecessary story text.

All questions:
- Keep all four options similar in length, grammar, and specificity.
- Avoid clues such as the longest option, extreme words, repeated wording, or unmatched units.
